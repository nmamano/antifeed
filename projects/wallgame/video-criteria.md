# How Nil reviews Wall Game daily videos

From the game-video app's review page and Nil's words on it (read 2026-10-30).
There are no past calls of his on video cards in Antifeed yet; once there
are, they count more than these lines.

A card is one finished daily video that waits in the review queue. It shows
the video, its caption (the post's text), the YouTube title when it has its
own, and a warning line for each sound with a high claim risk, for a video
that posts no Instagram Story, and when Approve cannot be taken now. His
call is one choice:

- approve: the video posts at the next slot, bound to this render and this
  caption.
- revise: back to the Wall Game Director with his note (a note is needed).
  It comes back for review only as a new render.
- discard: the video is dropped, with his note (a note is needed).

What matters to him (Nil, 2026-10-28): "I literally only need to see the
videos and captions. Everything else is context i don't need." So his call
rests on the video and the caption. He approved each of the first videos
that reached the review queue. He approves a sound with a high claim risk
or a video with no Story knowingly: those lines alone are not a reason to
revise. Approve is not possible while the card says it cannot be taken; then
the call is revise or discard, with a note.
