# How Nil decides the demo cards

Demo text for the walkthrough and for Nil's look at the page (2026-10-25).

Triage cards: one choice. go, design and hold take a priority, P0 (now) to
P3 (some day); backlog and obsolete take none. He raises visible bugs on
sign-in and checkout, and he sends nice-to-have features to the backlog
unless members asked for them.

Reply cards: send the draft, hold it, or skip the thread. He answers plain
date questions the same day, and holds any reply that promises a date that is
not final.
