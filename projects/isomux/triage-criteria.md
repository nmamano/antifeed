# How Nil triages Isomux tasks

From the isomux-curator skill and Nil's rulings in it (read 2026-10-25). His
past calls count more than these lines; read them through the past-calls
route (your input names it), not here.

A card is one open task in the Isomux room with no tag. Since Nil,
2026-10-30, it is the Loop app's card: the packet creator's pick (one
choice, a priority, a new title or scope when the task needs one), a why of
at most two sentences, and the task's evidence. Nil sees the pick, not your
Premove, and confirms it or changes the choice and the priority.

His call is one of the fixed answers: a choice and a priority. When you
predict that he takes the pick, copy its title and scope; when not, give
your own or null. Only the choice and the priority count.

The choices:

- go: the loop can do it start to finish.
- design: it needs his design call first; a worker researches it and sends a
  design packet.
- hold: the loop must never touch it: Stripe, Hosted, customers, secrets,
  releases, logins.
- backlog: P4; the loop never picks it.
- obsolete: close it; a merge already solved it, no current goal needs it, or
  nobody touched it for months. Obsolete is reversible, so his bar for it is
  low.

Priority is P0 (now) to P3 (some day) for go, design and hold; none for
backlog and obsolete. Most of his changes to a pick are priority changes,
and the reason is often not in the packet: his current goals.

- "why" is one sentence: why he makes this call.
- "against" is one sentence: your own doubt about your call, the one thing
  he should check. Name a choice or priority, or say the other course in
  full words; never point at a thing the card does not show.
- "why" and "against" are in a member's words: what a member sees and gets.
  No file names, paths, line numbers, code, commit hashes or task ids.

The packet shows the packet creator's pick, and Nil sees it too: he often
takes it. Predict his call from the task, the pick, the evidence and his
past calls.
